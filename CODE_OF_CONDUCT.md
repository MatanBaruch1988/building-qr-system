# Code of conduct

This project adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)
as its code of conduct. Read the full text there. In short:

## What we expect

- Be kind and respectful, also when you disagree.
- Be patient with people who are new to the project, to GitHub or to programming.
- Give feedback on the work, not on the person, and say what you suggest instead of only what is wrong.
- Accept feedback on your own work the same way, and thank the people who took the time to give it.
- Assume good faith, and ask when something is unclear before you decide what somebody meant.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, threats or personal attacks.
- Discrimination or demeaning jokes about a person's gender, sexuality, disability, age, appearance, race, ethnicity,
  nationality, language, religion or background.
- Publishing somebody's private information (a name, a phone number, an address, attendance records) without their
  permission.
- Spam, trolling, or pushing a point again and again after it was answered.
- Anything else that a reasonable person would find hostile in a professional setting.

## Be patient and clear

The people who use the app (committee members and service providers) are mostly not developers. Be patient, avoid
jargon where a plain word works, and explain what you mean. A bug report from somebody who has never opened an issue
before is as welcome as a pull request.

## Scope

This code of conduct applies to the project's spaces on GitHub: issues, pull requests, discussions, comments and
reviews.

## Reporting

Report it privately to the maintainer at [matan1988b+building-qr@gmail.com](mailto:matan1988b+building-qr@gmail.com).

The report is kept private, and the name of the person who reported is not shared without their consent.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit or remove content (comments, issues, pull requests), lock threads, or block an account when
the behavior breaks this code of conduct. The Contributor Covenant describes the possible consequences in more
detail.
